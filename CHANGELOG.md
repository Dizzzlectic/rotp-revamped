# Changelog

## 0.0.17

- Translate achievement titles, summaries, descriptions and objectives from kRO,
  using consistent place names and gameplay terms. Conflicting requirements
  retain Korean for manual review; IDs, counts and rewards remain unchanged.

## 0.0.16

- Translate all 11,517 pet dialogue entries across 135 kRO pet blocks,
  preserving each pet's personality, hunger states, action keys and repeated
  lines. Retain the approved kRO tutorial descriptions for Little Poring.

## 0.0.15

- Translate 118 Frost Joke lines and 116 Scream lines from kRO, adapting
  wordplay into natural English and preserving record order and repeats.

## 0.0.14

- Translate all 253 equipment random-option descriptions. Preserve option IDs,
  numeric placeholders and the END sentinel.
- Standardize stat labels and explanatory stat terminology across message
  strings.
- Use Critical for critical-chance labels and bonuses, Creativity for CRT, and
  iRO's division expression for race-specific critical bonuses.

## 0.0.13

- Use inline stat tooltip captions and green emphasis for better readability.

## 0.0.12

- Use Equipment Set and Unequip All for the equipment control labels.

## 0.0.11

- Shorten the equipment visibility label to Show Equip, matching iRO and
  ROenglishRE's compatibility CSV table.

## 0.0.10

- Rename the Special equipment tab to Costume, matching ROenglishRE. The tab
  includes costume and shadow equipment.

## 0.0.9

- Use General and Special for the equipment tabs. Special includes costume and
  shadow equipment.

## 0.0.8

- Use ROenglishRE's three-character inventory labels: Use, Eqp, Etc and Fav. The
  four-character labels still overflowed the vertical tabs.

## 0.0.7

- Shortened inventory tabs to Item, Gear, Etc. and Fav for the narrow inventory
  frame.

## 0.0.6

- Translated the complete kRO card prefix and suffix table. Preserved card IDs,
  record order and repeated entries; labels retain their placement before or
  after equipment names.

## 0.0.5

- Translated all text in the kRO signboard table, including service labels,
  travel destinations and level restrictions. Preserved signboard placement,
  image paths and display settings.

## 0.0.4

- Translated town and service labels, including shops, guides, inns and storage.
  Preserved kRO map IDs, coordinates and service types in both text modes.

## 0.0.3

- Translated the complete kRO client message table, including interface labels,
  command help and error messages. Preserved row positions, identifiers and
  formatting, with English command spellings and corrected numeric fields in the
  two retired account-time billing messages.

## 0.0.2

- Translated character title names from kRO, preserving title IDs, `VIP` and
  intentionally blank entries.

## 0.0.1

- Translated the complete native kRO map-name and map-info tables, including map
  labels, entry-banner titles and subtitles. Shared labels use consistent
  wording while retaining regional, directional and floor details.
- Preserved map identifiers, banner backgrounds and notification settings in the
  complete table overrides. These also apply in English translation mode.
